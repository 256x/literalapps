Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 5, 2026

This policy applies to all apps published under Literal Apps: Literal Agenda, Literal Memo, and Literal Launcher.

Data We Collect

We do not collect, store, or share any personal data. All app data (events, memos, settings) is stored locally on your device.

Internet Access

Literal Agenda and Literal Memo include an optional Git sync feature. If enabled, the app connects to a Git repository you specify and control (GitHub or a self-hosted Gitea instance). No data is sent to our servers — we have none.

Literal Launcher does not use the internet.

Third-Party Services

We do not use any analytics, advertising, or third-party SDKs.
